About the Innovation 🌸

CyberSakhi is a women safety product built to protect and empower girls using smart emergency technology. It provides SOS alerts, live tracking, voice-based activation, and evidence collection to ensure safety anytime, anywhere. 👩💗✨

Background of Product 💡

Created by student innovators to address real-world women safety concerns and to give every girl a reliable safety tool. 👑🔥
